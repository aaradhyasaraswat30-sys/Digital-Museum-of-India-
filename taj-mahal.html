The Taj Mahal

A timeless symbol of love, beauty and extraordinary architecture.

Introduction

The Taj Mahal is one of India's most famous historical monuments. Located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, it is admired around the world for its beautiful white marble structure, artistic details and peaceful surroundings. It is also recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

History

The Taj Mahal was commissioned by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Its construction began in 1632 and the main mausoleum was completed around 1648. Other parts of the complex were completed later.

Skilled craftsmen and workers from different regions contributed to building this magnificent monument.

Architecture

The Taj Mahal is a masterpiece of Mughal architecture. It is made mainly of white marble and features a large central dome, four minarets, beautiful arches and detailed floral decorations.

Its walls are decorated with delicate patterns and precious stones. The monument looks different under changing sunlight, making it especially beautiful throughout the day.

Interesting Facts

✦ The Taj Mahal is located on the banks of the Yamuna River.

✦ It was built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan.

✦ It became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983.

✦ Its white marble changes appearance with different lighting.

✦ It is considered one of the finest examples of Mughal architecture.

Legacy

Today, the Taj Mahal is a treasured part of India's cultural heritage. It attracts visitors from across the world and reminds us of the country's rich history, artistic skills and architectural achievements.
