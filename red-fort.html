The Red Fort

A magnificent symbol of India's history, courage and freedom.

Introduction

The Red Fort, also known as Lal Qila, is a famous historical monument located in Delhi, India. It is known for its massive red sandstone walls, beautiful buildings and impressive Mughal architecture. It is an important symbol of India's rich heritage.

History

The Red Fort was built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan. Construction began in 1638 when he decided to shift his capital from Agra to Delhi. The fort was completed in 1648.

It served as the main residence of Mughal emperors for many years. After British rule, it became an important symbol of India's independence. On 15 August every year, the Prime Minister of India hoists the national flag here.

Architecture

The Red Fort is famous for its strong red sandstone walls and beautiful Mughal-style buildings. It includes grand gates, gardens, halls and decorative structures.

Some important structures inside the fort include the Diwan-i-Aam, where the emperor addressed the public, and the Diwan-i-Khas, where private meetings were held.

Interesting Facts

✦ The Red Fort was built by Shah Jahan.

✦ It was completed in 1648.

✦ It is made mainly of red sandstone.

✦ It became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2007.

✦ The Prime Minister hoists India's national flag here every Independence Day.

Legacy

The Red Fort reminds us of India's glorious past and its journey towards independence. Today, it is one of the country's most treasured monuments and attracts visitors from across the world.
