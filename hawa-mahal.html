HAWA MAHAL

THE PALACE OF WINDS

A Palace Like No Other

Hawa Mahal is one of the most famous historical buildings in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Its name means "Palace of Winds". Built in 1799, this beautiful palace is known for its pink sandstone walls and hundreds of small windows. Its unique design makes it a remarkable example of Indian architecture.

The palace was commissioned by Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh and designed by architect Lal Chand Ustad. It was built as an extension of the royal city palace.

Photo: Prajeshdutta / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.

History

Hawa Mahal was constructed in 1799 during the reign of Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh. The design was inspired by the crown of Lord Krishna. It allowed royal women to observe everyday street life and festivals from behind the windows while maintaining privacy.

Did you know?

Hawa Mahal was built without a traditional foundation in the usual sense. Its curved shape and design help support the structure.

Architectural Beauty

Hawa Mahal is a five-storey building made mainly of red and pink sandstone. Its front has 953 small windows, known as jharokhas. These windows allow cool air to flow through the palace.

The building has delicate latticework, decorated balconies, domes and beautifully designed arches. Its special structure gives it a unique appearance.

Interesting Facts

1. The Palace of Winds

Its name comes from the cool breeze that passes through its many windows.

2. 953 Windows

The palace is famous for its 953 small windows, called jharokhas.

3. Five Storeys

Hawa Mahal has five beautifully designed floors.

4. Built in 1799

It was built during the rule of Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh.

A Lasting Legacy

Hawa Mahal continues to attract visitors from across the world. It represents the creativity, skill and artistic excellence of the builders of the past. Even today, it remains one of the most recognisable symbols of Jaipur and India's rich heritage.
